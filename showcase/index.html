Zustand Inspector Playground

Three zustand/vanilla stores, no React, inspected inside TanStack Devtools with a panel built from Meta's Astryx components. Everything below the cards is the real plugin.

  1. Click +, Slow +1 and Refresh
  2. Filter by Slow or Wasted
  3. Click a state key to see who changed it
  4. Click Compare, then pick two actions
